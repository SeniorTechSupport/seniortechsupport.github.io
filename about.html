About Senior Technology Support

A student-founded volunteer program helping older adults become more confident with technology through patient, one-on-one support.

Meet Aiden

Hi, I'm Aiden O'Neill, a high school senior at University Preparatory Academy in San Jose, CA, and the founder of Senior Technology Support. I started this project after seeing how a little patience and clear instruction could make technology feel less overwhelming for older adults. Since then, I've volunteered with older relatives, neighbors, and residents of an independent and assisted living community in San Jose, helping them become more confident using the technology they already own.

I also designed and built this website so seniors and their families can access technology guides, troubleshooting resources, and request help between in-person sessions.

Why I Started It

Over and over, I noticed the same thing: most technology isn't actually difficult—it just feels unfamiliar at first. What helps isn't doing it for someone, but meeting people where they are and explaining each step clearly, patiently, and without judgment.

Senior Technology Support exists to provide calm, patient support and easy-to-follow resources that help older adults become more independent and confident using technology.

How I Try to Teach

I've learned that most technology problems come from one missed step or one unfamiliar setting—not from a lack of ability. That's why I slow down, explain every step clearly, and make sure each person understands how to solve the problem independently. My goal isn't just to fix a device—it's to build confidence.

Beyond In-Person Visits

Senior Technology Support is more than an in-person help session. I designed and built this website so older adults, their families, and community members can keep learning through step-by-step technology guides and accessibility resources, and can request help remotely whenever they need it — all using the same patient, step-by-step approach I bring to every volunteer session.
